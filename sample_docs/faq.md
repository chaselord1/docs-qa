# Northwind Bike Co. Shop FAQ

## Do you offer assembly for bikes bought online?
Yes. Every bike shipped from our online store includes free basic assembly
if you bring it to any Northwind Bike Co. location within 30 days of
delivery. Walk-ins are welcome, but booking a slot online is faster.

## How long does a tune-up take?
A standard tune-up takes about 90 minutes while you wait, or you can drop
the bike off and pick it up the next day. During peak season (April
through August) drop-off turnaround can run two to three days.

## Do you price match?
We match any listed price from another local bike shop within 15 miles,
as long as the bike is the same model, size, and color. Online-only
retailers are not eligible for price matching.

## Can I trade in my old bike?
We accept trade-ins on bikes less than 10 years old that are rideable
without major repair. Trade-in value is store credit only, applied at
checkout, and is assessed in person by shop staff.

## What sizes of kids bikes do you carry?
We stock wheel sizes from 12 inch through 24 inch for kids bikes. Anything
above 24 inch is treated as an adult frame and sized by height instead of
wheel diameter.

## Do you rent bikes?
Yes, at our downtown and riverside locations only. Rentals are by the hour
or by the day, and a valid ID is required as a deposit hold.

## What is your loaner bike policy for repairs?
If a repair is expected to take longer than three business days, ask the
service desk about a loaner. Loaners are subject to availability and a
refundable $40 hold on a card.

## Do you offer group rides?
Yes, every Saturday morning at 8am starting from the downtown location,
weather permitting. All skill levels are welcome and helmets are required.
