# Northwind Bike Co. Returns and Warranty Policy

## Returns
Unridden bikes and accessories can be returned within 30 days of purchase
for a full refund to the original payment method, provided you have the
receipt and the item is in original packaging. Ridden bikes cannot be
returned for a refund, but see the exchange section below.

A bike is considered "ridden" once it has left the store on its wheels or
been assembled and test-ridden outside the fitting area. Sitting on a bike
in the showroom does not count as ridden.

## Exchanges
If a bike doesn't fit once you get it home, bring it back within 14 days
for a size or model exchange, even if it has been ridden a short distance.
Exchanges are limited to one per purchase and any price difference is
charged or refunded at the time of exchange.

## Warranty coverage
Bike frames carry a limited lifetime warranty against manufacturing
defects to the original owner. This covers cracks or failures from normal
riding, not damage from crashes, misuse, or unauthorized modification.

Components (brakes, drivetrain, wheels) carry the manufacturer's standard
warranty, typically one to two years depending on the part. Wear items
like tires, brake pads, and chains are never covered under warranty.

## How to file a warranty claim
Bring the bike and your original receipt to any Northwind Bike Co.
location. Staff will inspect the frame or part in person; we do not
process warranty claims by phone or email. Approved claims are repaired
or replaced at no cost; if the original part is discontinued, we
substitute the closest current equivalent.

## Refund timing
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7
business days. Store credit refunds, when chosen instead of a card refund,
are available immediately.

## What voids the warranty
Warranty coverage is void if the bike has been modified with
non-original parts affecting frame integrity, used for commercial
purposes such as delivery or rental without prior written approval, or
damaged in a crash or collision.
